# P7. An analytic proof of the first semilocal step

Window positivity with only the prime 2, on windows below log 3, is certified by computer (margin 5.54e-8; also covered by Zhu).
Connes and Consani proved the no-prime (archimedean) case analytically on support log 2. Give an analytic mechanism for the first prime:
the structure is known (one archimedean negative level, odd about log sqrt 2, lifted by the reflection B_2 = 2 R_b; the even second level
caps the weight; positivity holds only at the exact phase of the prime). A mechanism here would be the first explanation, not just a
certificate, of semilocal positivity.
